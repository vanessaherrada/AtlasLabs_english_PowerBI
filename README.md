# ATLAS LABS – HR Analytics Dashboard 📊

Power BI case study presenting headcount, demographics, performance and attrition views for Atlas Labs’ workforce. All content below is pulled directly from the report pages shown in the screenshots you shared—no extra assumptions.

## 🚀 Quick start

1. Clone the repository

2. Open the report
Launch Case Study HR Atlas Labs.pbix in Power BI Desktop – the data is already embedded, so it opens straight away.

## 📄 Report pages & highlights

### Overview	
• KPI cards – 1470 Total Employees, 1233 Active, 237 Inactive, 16.1 % Attrition Rate 
• Column chart Employee Hiring Trends (2012‑2022) split by attrition 
• Bar Active Employees by Department & treemap Department → Job Role

### Demographics	

• Youngest employee 18 yrs, oldest 51 yrs 
• Donut Employees by Marital Status 
• Combo Employees by Ethnicity & Average Salary • Stacked bar Employees by Age & Gender • Bar Employees by Age

### Performance Tracker	

• Employee slicer (example: Abra MacGray) 
• Start 14 Mar 2021, Last Review 07 Jul 2022, Next Review 07 Jul 2023 
• Six line charts tracking satisfaction & ratings (2014‑2022)

### Attrition	

• KPI card Attrition Rate 16.1 % 
• Line Attrition Rate by Year 
• Stacked column % Attrition by Department & Job Role 
• Bar/line Attrition by Travel Frequency 
• Bar Attrition by Overtime Requirement 
• Histogram Attrition by Tenure
